FAQ

Dispatch Questions

How much does Fair Miles charge for dispatch?

5% of linehaul, or a flat $250 per truck per week — whichever you choose. There is no setup fee.

What do I need to sign up for dispatch under my own MC?

An active FMCSA authority (MC and USDOT numbers in good standing), active cargo and liability insurance at broker-standard limits, and a clean safety record.

Do I keep my own customers and rates?

Yes. You keep your MC, your insurance, and your customer relationships. Fair Miles books from the open market and negotiates rates on every load.

Is dispatch support available outside business hours?

Yes, dispatch support is available 24/7 for breakdowns, detention, and anything that can't wait.
